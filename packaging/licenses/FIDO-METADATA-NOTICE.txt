FIDO Alliance Metadata Service (MDS)

MDS data is downloaded separately at runtime. It is not part of KeyMelier's
MIT-licensed source or its open-source dependency licenses. FIDO and the
contributing authenticator vendors retain rights in this data.

Use is subject to the applicable FIDO Metadata Usage Terms:
Developers: https://fidoalliance.org/metadata-usage-terms-for-developers/
Users/relying parties: https://fidoalliance.org/metadata-usage-terms-for-relying-parties-or-service-providers-2/

Those terms limit use to supporting authentication under FIDO specifications.
They require preservation of legal headers, verification of BLOB signatures,
and removal of statements withdrawn from the latest BLOB. They include
warranty disclaimers and liability limitations; consult the linked terms.
KeyMelier does not grant a separate license to redistribute MDS statements.

KeyMelier uses MDS for local authenticator checks. It preserves the signed
BLOB, including legal headers, in a replaceable local cache. It does not ship
the BLOB or put MDS model metadata in new history/sync/CSV exports. Icons from
current verified statements identify authenticators in the application, as
described by the Metadata Statement specification's icon field:
https://fidoalliance.org/specs/mds/fido-metadata-statement-v3.1-ps-20250521.html#metadata-keys
This use does not imply that a vendor sponsors or endorses KeyMelier.
Unavailable or expired verification evidence
cannot establish authenticity. A result is a technical observation, not a
guarantee by KeyMelier, FIDO or a contributing vendor.
